# Card image shot list for Resale Tracker

Put raw front card images in `assets/card_images/`.

Use **one image per card**. The same image is used for all grade levels unless you add a grade-specific file.
PNG, JPG, JPEG, and WebP all work.
Recommended format: portrait card images around 600x840, 750x1050, or 900x1260.

The app checks in this order:

```text
player-card-gGRADE.png
player-card.png
player.png
```

So for your setup, use the `player-card.png` filename.
